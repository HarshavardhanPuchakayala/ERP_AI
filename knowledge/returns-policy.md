# Returns Policy

## Return window

Goods may be returned within 14 days of delivery if they are unused and in the original packaging. Requests after 14 days are reviewed case by case by the sales manager.

## Restocking fee

Returns of undamaged goods carry a 10 percent restocking fee. The fee is waived when the return is due to our error or when the goods arrive defective.

## Defective or damaged goods

Report defects within 7 days of delivery with photos. We will replace the goods or issue a credit note. Credit notes reduce the customer's outstanding balance.

## Items that cannot be returned

Custom-made items, opened paint and chemical products, and items marked clearance are final sale.
