# Credit Policy

## Payment terms

Standard terms are net 30: an invoice is due 30 days after the invoice date. New customers start with a credit limit of 50,000 and may be raised after six months of on-time payments.

## Overdue invoices

An invoice is overdue the day after its due date. The collections steps are:
- Day 1 overdue: send a friendly payment reminder by email.
- Day 7 overdue: send a second reminder and phone the customer's accounts contact.
- Day 15 overdue: escalate to the sales manager for a personal call.
- Day 30 overdue: place the customer on credit hold. No new orders are shipped until the balance is cleared.

## Late fees

A late fee of 1.5 percent per month applies to balances more than 30 days overdue. The sales manager may waive a late fee once per customer per year.

## Credit hold

A customer on credit hold can still pay and ask questions. The hold is lifted by the finance team after the overdue balance is paid in full.
